The flywheel

Trades buy land. Land sells higher. Profit buys more land.

Yield Fields Strategy turns trading volume into a growing farm of Yield Field Deeds. Each turn of the wheel feeds the next one.

  1. 1

    Trade $YFS

    Every buy and every sell on PONS carries a 5% tax.

  2. 2

    The barn fills up

    The tax collects in the strategy treasury, trade by trade.

  3. 3

    Buy Deeds at the floor

    When the barn holds enough, it buys Yield Field Deeds at the floor price.

  4. 4

    Relist higher

    Every Deed the strategy owns goes back on the market above the price it paid.

  5. 5

    Sell and replant

    When a Deed sells, the proceeds return to the barn and buy the next ones. The wheel spins again, a little bigger.

FAQ

Questions from the farmhouse

What is Yield Fields Strategy?

A token on Robinhood Chain with a 5% tax on buys and sells. The tax is used to buy Yield Field Deeds, relist them above cost, and recycle the proceeds into more Deeds.

Where does the 5% tax go?

Into the strategy treasury, which we call the barn. The barn's only job is buying Yield Field Deeds and selling them for more than it paid.

Why Yield Field Deeds?

Deeds are the land of Yield Fields, a limited set of 3,333 plots. Land is what every farmer in the game needs, which makes it a natural asset for the flywheel to collect.

Do I need to own a Deed or play the game?

No. Holding $YFS is enough. You get exposure to the strategy without buying a Deed yourself.

How do I buy?

Bridge some ETH to Robinhood Chain, open the token on PONS and swap. Always check that the contract address matches the one posted on this site and on our X account.

Is this the official Yield Fields token?

No. Yield Fields Strategy is an independent community project and is not run or endorsed by the Yield Fields team.
